#### Context

During team development, administrators can manage teams, such as creating teams and managing team members.

You can create sub-teams under a team.

-   Three sub-teams can be created.
-   Upper-level team users can manage lower-level teams.
-   Upper-level team users can view projects of lower-level teams.
