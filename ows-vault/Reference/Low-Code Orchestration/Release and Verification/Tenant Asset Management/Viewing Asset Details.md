#### Context

Asset sources are as follows:

Local assets

-   Assets uploaded on the **Asset Management** page. These assets are displayed on the **Local Assets** tab page. For details, see [[Uploading Assets to the Asset Management Page|Uploading Assets to the Asset Management Page]].
-   Packaged apps, project files, templates, and service components, and saved card assets. These assets are displayed on the **Local Assets** tab page.

Putaway assets

-   Assets pulled from GDE Store. For details, see [[Subscribing to an Asset|Subscribing to an Asset]].
-   Assets that are put away through the pipeline, studio, and asset management. These assets are displayed on the **Putaway Assets** tab page. For details, see [[Putting Away Assets to the Asset Management Page|Putting Away Assets to the Asset Management Page]].
