#### Procedure

1.  Log in to the GDE data zone portal as the **admin** user.
    
    -   When the portal is displayed in shortcut mode, choose **Common Management**, and click **Development State Studio**.
    -   When the portal is displayed in menu mode, choose **Products and Services** > **Development State Studio** on the home page.
    
2.  On the menu bar of the home page, choose **Asset Management** > **Asset Library**.
3.  Click the **Local Assets** tab.
    
    ![[note_3.0-en-us.png]]
    
    The method for filtering putaway assets is the same as that for filtering local assets. The following uses local assets as an example.
    
4.  In the list on the left, select a label under **Service Domain**. Assets that meet the filter criteria are displayed on the right.
    
    ![[en-us_image_0000001568659022.png]]
    
5.  In the list on the left, select a label under **Asset Type**. Assets that meet the filter criteria are displayed on the right.
    
    ![[en-us_image_0000001618899169.png]]
    
6.  In the **Technical Category** drop-down list, further filter the type. The asset type is associated with a technology category. They can be used together to filter assets of a specified type.
    
    ![[en-us_image_0000001568818942.png]]
