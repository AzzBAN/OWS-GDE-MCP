#### Procedure

1.  Log in to the GDE data zone portal as the **admin** user.
    
    -   When the portal is displayed in shortcut mode, choose **Common Management**, and click **Development State Studio**.
    -   When the portal is displayed in menu mode, choose **Products and Services** > **Development State Studio** on the home page.
    
2.  On the menu bar of the home page, choose **Asset Management** > **Asset Library**.
3.  Click the **Local Assets** tab.
4.  Click ![[en-us_image_0000001618966377.png]] on the right of **Custom** in the **Asset Catalog** area. The **Edit Asset Catalog** page is displayed.
5.  Associate the asset catalog with a technical category.
    
    **Figure 1** Associating the asset catalog with a technical category  
    ![[en-us_image_0000001618805733.png]]
    
6.  Click **Save**.
