# Subscribing to an Asset

GDE Store is used to put away and share assets. Developers can download required assets from GDE Store and share their apps within GDE Store.

After ADC is interworked with GDE Store, you can obtain assets from remote or local GDE Store for asset management.

![[note_3.0-en-us.png]]

Before subscribing to an asset, you need to be bound with the domain administrator permission of the service domain to which the asset belongs. For details, see [[Managing Service Domain Labels|Managing Service Domain Labels]].

-   **[[Subscribing to Assets from the Remote GDE Store|Subscribing to Assets from the Remote GDE Store]]**  
    The **Asset Management** page allows you to subscribe to assets from a remote GDE Store.
-   **[[Subscribing to Assets from the Local GDE Store|Subscribing to Assets from the Local GDE Store]]**  
    The **Asset Management** page allows you to subscribe to assets from the local GDE Store.
