# Putting Away Assets to the Asset Management Page

Developers can put away assets through a pipeline or using the asset management page.

-   **[[Putting Away Assets Through the Pipeline|Putting Away Assets Through the Pipeline]]**  
    Package the projects that are edited online and put away them to the **Asset Management** page.
-   **[[Putting Away an Asset on the Asset Management Page|Putting Away an Asset on the Asset Management Page]]**  
    Put away the assets on the **Local Assets** tab page for other users to use.
