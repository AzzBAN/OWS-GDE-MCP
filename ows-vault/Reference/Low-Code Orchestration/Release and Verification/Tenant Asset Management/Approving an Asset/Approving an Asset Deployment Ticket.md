#### Prerequisites

-   The system administrator of the develop-state environment has been bound to the permissions of the O&M administrator role **AssetMaintenanceAdmin**.
-   The asset deployment ticket viewing permission is described as follows:
    -   Historical tickets that are upgraded to the latest version and have no service domain can be viewed by all users.
    -   Asset deployment tickets whose source is **Tenant asset service** do not have service domains and can be viewed by all users.
    -   Asset deployment tickets whose source is **Customer asset library** and that have service domains can be viewed by the domain administrator and develop-state system administrator.
