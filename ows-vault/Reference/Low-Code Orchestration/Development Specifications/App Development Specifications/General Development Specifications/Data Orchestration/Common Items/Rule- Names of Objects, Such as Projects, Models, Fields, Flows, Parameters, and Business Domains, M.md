# Rule: Names of Objects, Such as Projects, Models, Fields, Flows, Parameters, and Business Domains, Must Have Clear Business Meanings

**Specification name**: General\_DataFactory\_Common\_Naming\_Conventions

**Description**: During app development, the business side needs to formulate a set of business scenario–based naming rules for objects, such as projects, models, flows, parameters, and business domains, to reflect business meanings or application scenarios. The names must be self-explanatory.

**Check guide**:

Check whether the names of the objects can reflect business meanings or application scenarios.

**Negative example**:

**AAA.TEST** or **OO.XXX**

**Positive example**:

**SEQ.CELL\_STATISTICS** or **OWS.TRAFFIC\_ANALYZE**

**Impact**: Non-standard names make it difficult to maintain assets.
