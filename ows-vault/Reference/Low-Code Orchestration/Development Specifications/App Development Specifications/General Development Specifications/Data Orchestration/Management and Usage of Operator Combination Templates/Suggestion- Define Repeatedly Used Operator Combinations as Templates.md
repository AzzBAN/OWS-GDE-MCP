# Suggestion: Define Repeatedly Used Operator Combinations as Templates

**Specification name**: General\_DataFactory\_Operator\_Combination\_Define\_Reuse\_Combination

**Description**: During flow orchestration, if similar or even identical operator combinations are repeatedly used, you can abstract the operator combinations and define them as templates, so that those templates can be quickly referenced and used after simple configuration.

**Positive example**: In a service scenario, if the Filter and Group operators are often used together and the service logic is the same, you can define the Filter and Group operators as a template.
