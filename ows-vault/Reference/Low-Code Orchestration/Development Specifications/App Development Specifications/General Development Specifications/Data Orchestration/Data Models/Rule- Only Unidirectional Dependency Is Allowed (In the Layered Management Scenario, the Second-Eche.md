# Rule: Only Unidirectional Dependency Is Allowed (In the Layered Management Scenario, the Second-Echelon Apps Can Depend On the First-Echelon Apps, but the First-Echelon Apps Cannot Depend On the Second-Echelon Apps)

**Specification name**: General\_DataFactory\_Data\_Model\_Dependency\_Principle

**Description**: In the layered management scenario, the second-echelon apps can depend on the first-echelon apps, the third-echelon apps can depend on the first- and second-echelon apps, but the third-echelon apps cannot be depended on. The theme models are used as the basic models and placed in the first echelon. The object models are developed based on the theme models and placed in the second echelon. The models of these two types are used as basic packages to form domain assets and referenced by the third-echelon apps. Apps in the third echelon cannot depend on each other. The common models must be placed in the first and second echelons.

**Check guide**: Check whether the app depends on apps in the same or lower echelon.

**Negative example**: An app in the third echelon depends on SDRs generated by another app in the same echelon.

**Positive example**: An app in the third echelon depends only on the second-echelon apps.

**Impact**: If the unidirectional dependency is violated, cyclic dependency may occur, causing an app installation failure.
