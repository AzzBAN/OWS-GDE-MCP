# PC Card Development Guide

A card is a combination of page elements and functions with certain business significance. It consists of technical components and business services and is used to display data and information.

    ADC enables you to create cards online. This section describes how to create a PC card.
-   **[[Configuring a PC Card Component|Configuring a PC Card Component]]**
