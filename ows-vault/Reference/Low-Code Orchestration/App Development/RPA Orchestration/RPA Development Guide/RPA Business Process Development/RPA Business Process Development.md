# RPA Business Process Development

After learning how to develop RPA business processes, you can master common Studio operations.

-   **[[RPA Business Development Process|RPA Business Development Process]]**  
    This section describes how to develop an RPA business process.
-   **[[Creating a Project|Creating a Project]]**  
    Studio allows you to create a project manually or using a template.
-   **[[Recording Robotic Functional Blocks or Scripts|Recording Robotic Functional Blocks or Scripts]]**  
    This section describes how to record robotic functional blocks or scripts.
-   **[[Orchestrating an RPA Business Process|Orchestrating an RPA Business Process]]**  
    After the robotic script is recorded, orchestrate an RPA business process to realize business automation.
-   **[[Saving an RPA Business Process|Saving an RPA Business Process]]**  
    After you orchestrate an RPA business process, save the process to prevent data loss.
-   **[[Checking an RPA Business Process|Checking an RPA Business Process]]**  
    After the orchestrated RPA business process is saved, perform health check on the business process.
-   **[[Debugging an RPA Business Process|Debugging an RPA Business Process]]**  
    After the RPA business process is checked, debug it to verify that each step in the process is available.
-   **[[Running an RPA Business Process|Running an RPA Business Process]]**  
    After the RPA business process is debugged, run the entire process to check whether it is correct.
-   **[[Releasing an RPA Business Process|Releasing an RPA Business Process]]**  
    After the RPA business process is debugged and running properly, release it to RPA Orchestrator, GDE Store, or a local disk.
