# RPA Business Development Process

This section describes how to develop an RPA business process.

[Figure 1](#EN-US_TOPIC_0000001207716063__fig11315933916) shows how to develop an RPA business process.

**Figure 1** Development process  
![[en-us_image_0000001171706554.png]]

**Table 1** Process description   
| Step | Sub-step | Description |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| Project Creation | Creating a Project | Create a project before developing an RPA business process. |
| RPA Business Process Development | Recording Robotic Functional Blocks or Scripts | Record robot functional blocks or scripts for orchestrating the RPA business process based on site requirements. |
| Orchestrating an RPA Business Process | Orchestrate RPA business process elements such as function blocks, scripts, and controls based on site requirements. |
| Saving an RPA Business Process | Save the orchestrated RPA business process to prevent data loss. |
| RPA Business Process Verification | Checking an RPA Business Process | After the RPA business process is successfully saved, perform health check on the RPA business process. |
| Debugging an RPA Business Process | Debug the saved RPA business process to check whether it works properly. If no, locate the fault, analyze the fault cause, and rectify the fault. |
| Running an RPA Business Process | Run the saved RPA business process to ensure that the entire process can be executed. |
| RPA Business Process Release | Releasing an RPA Business Process | Release the verified RPA business process to RPA Orchestrator or your local disk. | **Parent topic:** [[RPA Business Process Development|RPA Business Process Development]]
