#### Basic Concepts

-   Control: corresponds to a step in the RPA business process. For example, you can use the **Open Web Page** control to open a specified web page.
-   Functional Block: corresponds to a sub-process in the RPA business process. Multiple steps are encapsulated into a functional block during recording for the RPA business process to use.
-   Subscript: corresponds to a sub-process in the RPA business process. Multiple steps are stored in a subscript during recording for the RPA business process to call.
