#### Procedure

1.  Log in to the home page of the agent, click ![[en-us_image_0000002333132245.png]] in the upper right corner, and choose **Settings**.
2.  In the navigation pane, choose **License**.
3.  Click ![[en-us_image_0000001446442074.png]] in the lower right corner of **ESN** to copy the ESN of the current PC and record it on the local PC for future use.
    
    ![[notice_3.0-en-us.png]]
    
    ESN is the device identifier of the RPA agent and is not associated with a personal account.
    
4.  Click ![[en-us_image_0000001496641993.png]] in the lower right corner of **Official website link** to go to the RPA download page for trial use.
5.  Enter the ESN obtained in [3](#EN-US_TOPIC_0000001734927890__en-us_topic_0000001158160681_li1276017469918) in the **ESN** text box and set **Product Name** to **Robot Assistant**.
6.  Click **Get License** to download the license file.
7.  Go back to the **Apply License** page of Studio, and click **Re-import** to import the downloaded license file.
8.  (Optional) To delete the imported license file, click **Remove**. In the displayed dialog box, click **Confirm**.
