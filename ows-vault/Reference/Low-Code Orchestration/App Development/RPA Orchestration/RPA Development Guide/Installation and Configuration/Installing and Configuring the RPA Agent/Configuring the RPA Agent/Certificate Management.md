#### Procedure

1.  Log in to the home page of the agent, click ![[en-us_image_0000002299093050.png]] in the upper right corner, and choose **Settings**.
2.  In the navigation pane, choose **Certificate Management**.
3.  Click **Import Certificate**.
4.  In the displayed dialog box, click **Browse** next to **Path**, select the path for storing the certificate, and enter the alias of the certificate.
    
    **Figure 1** Importing a certificate  
    ![[en-us_image_0000001307922093.png]]
    
5.  Click **Next**.
6.  In the displayed dialog box, verify that the certificate is correct and click **Finish**.
    
    ![[en-us_image_0000001239308598.png]]
    
7.  After the import is complete, check whether the imported certificate exists on the certificate list page.
