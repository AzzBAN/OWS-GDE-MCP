#### Procedure

1.  Log in to the home page of the agent, click ![[en-us_image_0000002333253517.png]] in the upper right corner, and choose **Settings**.
2.  In the navigation pane, choose **Credential Settings**.
3.  Click **Configure** on the right of **Password**.
4.  In the **Credential Settings** dialog box, enter the PiP credential password.
    
    **Figure 1** Entering the PiP credential password
    
    ![[en-us_image_0000001811010110.png]]
    
5.  Click **OK**.
