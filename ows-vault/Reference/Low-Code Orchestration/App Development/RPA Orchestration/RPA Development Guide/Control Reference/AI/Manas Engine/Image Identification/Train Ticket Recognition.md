# Train Ticket Recognition

**getTrainTicketInfoOcr**

**Description:**

OCR recognizes the train ticket picture online and returns the data information in the picture: Supports structured recognition of the key fields of the blue train ticket, including the ticket number, departure station, destination station, train number, date, fare, seat, etc. Name, seat number, ID number, sales station, serial number, time, etc.; the entered train ticket picture must be complete and clear in order to improve the recognition accuracy; method of obtaining user-id and user-key: See https://connect.huaweicloud.com/courses/learn/Learning/sp:cloudEdu\_?courseNo=course-v1:HuaweiX+CBUCNXEA006+Self-paced&courseType=1, and see Section 3.2 of Chapter 3 of the course.

**Input: none**
