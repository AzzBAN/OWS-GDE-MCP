# VAT Invoice Recognition

**getVatInfoOcr**

**Description:**

OCR recognizes value-added tax invoices online and returns the data information in the picture:

Supports structured identification of all key fields of general value-added tax invoices, special invoices, and electronic invoices, including basic invoice information, seller and buyer information, and product information , Price and tax information, etc.;

The input invoice picture must be kept complete and clear to improve the recognition accuracy;

How to obtain the user-id and user-key: See https://connect.huaweicloud.com/courses/learn/Learning/sp:cloudEdu\_?courseNo=course-v1:HuaweiX+CBUCNXEA006+Self-paced&courseType=1, and see Section 3.2 of Chapter 3 of the course.

**Input: none**
