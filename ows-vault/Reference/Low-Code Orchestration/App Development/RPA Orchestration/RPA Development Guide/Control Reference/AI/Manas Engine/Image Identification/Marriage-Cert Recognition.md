# Marriage-Cert Recognition

**getMarriageCertificateInfoOcr**

**Description:**

OCR recognizes the marriage certificate picture online and returns the data information in the picture:

supports the structured recognition of the key fields of the marriage certificate of the People\`s Republic of China, including the holder, the date of registration, and the marriage certificate number;

the entered marriage certificate picture must be kept intact , Clear in order to improve recognition accuracy;

method to obtain user-id and user-key: See https://connect.huaweicloud.com/courses/learn/Learning/sp:cloudEdu\_?courseNo=course-v1:HuaweiX+CBUCNXEA006+Self-paced&courseType=1, and see Section 3.2 of Chapter 3 of the course.

**Input: none**
