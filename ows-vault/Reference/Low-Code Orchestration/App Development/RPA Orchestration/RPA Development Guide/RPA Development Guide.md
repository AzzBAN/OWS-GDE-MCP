# RPA Development Guide

This guide describes how to use RPA Studio to develop RPA business processes, including installing RPA Studio, developing RPA business processes, and developing customized plugins.

-   **[[Quick Start|Quick Start]]**  
    If you are new to Robotic Process Automation (RPA), focus on basic RPA concepts, identify the RPA functionalities, and then develop the first instance to help you quickly get started with RPA.
-   **[[Installation and Configuration|Installation and Configuration]]**  
    After RPA tools are learnt and Studio is installed and configured, you can use Studio to develop robotic processes.
-   **[[RPA Business Process Development|RPA Business Process Development]]**  
    After learning how to develop RPA business processes, you can master common Studio operations.
-   **[[Customized Plugin Development|Customized Plugin Development]]**  
    Studio allows you to customize plugins (controls) for RPA business processes based on site requirements.
-   **[[Developing a Customized Recorder|Developing a Customized Recorder]]**  
    Studio allows you to customize a recorder for a specified application based on service requirements.
-   **[[Control Reference|Control Reference]]**  
    This section describes the controls supported by Studio and control properties.
-   **[[Appendix|Appendix]]**  
    This section describes the reference information and FAQs for using Studio.
