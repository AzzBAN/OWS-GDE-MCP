#### Procedure

1.  On the project list page, click the created **HyperAutoProcess** project.
2.  In the navigation pane, choose **Business Process**.
3.  In the process list, click ![[en-us_image_0000001599803626.png]].
4.  On the **Business Process** tab page, choose **More** > **Debug** in the **Operation** column of the HyperAutoProcess process.
5.  Click ![[en-us_image_0000001599803794.png]] in the upper part of the canvas to start debugging.
6.  On the displayed page, configure the information required by the ticket.
    
    ![[en-us_image_0000001649284581.png]]
    
7.  Click **Submit**.
8.  On the debugging page, perform the following operations:
    
    -   Click a phase in the process diagram and click the **Property** tab to view the properties of the phase.
    -   Click an instance in the instance list on the left to view the executed variable information on the right. Select a variable to view its value. If the variable is complex, expand it to view its value.
